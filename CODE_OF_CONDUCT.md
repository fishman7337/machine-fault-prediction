# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and outcomes.
- Prefer clear technical evidence over personal judgment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Public or private abuse.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of technical discussion.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should include enough context for maintainers to understand what happened and respond fairly.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and related community channels.
